WHAT'S IN THE APK

Built like a game. Useful like a real map.

A 2.4-metre block grid

Every road, river, park, and building footprint snaps to a global block grid. Zoom out for a continent view, or zoom all the way in to see individual blocks, building shadows, and trees.

Full-screen Android Auto

MineMaps sits in Android Auto's main map slot, just like Google Maps or Waze. Run it full screen or split-screen next to Spotify, and it switches to night blocks when your headlights come on.

Routes, Google Maps & radars

Get free turn-by-turn routes via OpenStreetMap, or link with Google Maps to mirror live turn banners on top of the block map. Includes dynamic driving zoom and flashing speed-camera alerts.

34 pixel-art bookmark icons

Long-press any spot to save it with a banner, diamond, bed, chest, potion, or sword. Your saved places sync to the car screen and can be exported to a JSON file whenever you switch phones.

Fast search with distance

An on-device index searches nearby towns, streets, and shops in under 150 ms, showing how far away each result is. Non-Latin place names are transliterated into English letters automatically.

Offline cache & self-updates

Areas you've looked at stay cached on your phone (up to 200 MB for 30 days) for dead zones. When a new release drops on GitHub, the app checks the SHA-256 hash and updates itself.
