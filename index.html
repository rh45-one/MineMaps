EVERYTHING YOU NEED

Built Like a Game. Useful Like a Real Map.

Live Global Block World

Zoom smoothly from a world overview down to individual 2.4-metre blocks with building shadows, water depth, and forest canopies.

Full-Screen Android Auto

Takes Android Auto's main map slot — run it full screen or side-by-side with Spotify and music apps in day or night mode.

Standalone or Google Assisted

Draw pixel-art routes with free OpenStreetMap routing, mirror live Google Maps turn notifications, or plug in your own Google API key.

34 Item Bookmark Icons

Long-press anywhere to save home, work, or favourite spots with banners, diamonds, beds, chests, potions, swords, and cakes.

Instant Search & Live Distance

On-device place indexing finds nearby towns, streets, and landmarks in milliseconds with live distance badges and English names worldwide.

Offline Cache & Auto-Updates

Visited areas stay cached for 30 days (up to 200 MB). Built-in updater verifies SHA-256 checksums and keeps your custom textures intact.
